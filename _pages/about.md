## About

I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), advised by **Professor Junxian He**. My research focuses on **natural language processing (NLP)** and **machine learning**, with particular interests in:

- **LLM Reasoning and Reinforcement Learning**
- **Hallucination in Vision-Language Models (VLM)**
- **LLM Truthfulness and Interpretability**

I received my **B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU)** in June 2024. During my undergraduate studies I was advised by Professor Junxian He, and I received the **Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship at SJTU**.
